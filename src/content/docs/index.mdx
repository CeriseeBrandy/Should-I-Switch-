## What is this site?

**ShouldISwitch** is not a Linux fan site.

It's a resource built to give you a realistic answer to one question: *is Linux actually a good fit for you, right now, for what you actually do?*

Linux is powerful, free, and genuinely excellent — for the right person, in the right situation. For others, switching means frustration, broken workflows, and eventually going back. This guide helps you figure out which camp you're in **before** you do anything irreversible.

## Choosing a Distribution

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="🟡 Beginner-Friendly" icon="approve-check">
    Ubuntu, Linux Mint, and Pop!_OS. Good documentation, broad hardware support, friendly defaults.

    [See beginner distros →](/distros/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="⚙️ Developer-Oriented" icon="setting">
    Fedora, openSUSE. Slightly more technical, but stable and closer to enterprise Linux workflows.

    [See developer distros →](/distros/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="🔒 Stable & Long-Term" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Debian, Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux. Built for longevity. Excellent for servers.

    [See server distros →](/distros/)
  </Card>

  <Card title="⚠️ Advanced Only" icon="warning">
    Arch, NixOS, Gentoo. Powerful and customizable — not recommended as a first Linux experience.
